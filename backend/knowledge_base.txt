# Olist AI Business Intelligence - Knowledge Base

## Project Overview
This system analyzes Olist Brazilian e-commerce data to support business decisions using SQL analytics, demand forecasting, product trend analysis, anomaly detection, opportunity scoring, and risk scoring.

## Dataset
The project uses the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset.

## Business Metrics
Revenue for ML/business analysis is based on delivered orders and product item price.
AOV means average order value.
Demand means the number of product units sold in a period.

## Demand Forecasting
The forecasting system predicts future product demand using historical product-level demand features.
The final forecast currently targets September 2018.
Forecast quality was evaluated using leakage-free time-based validation.

## Forecast Interpretation
forecast_demand = predicted product demand.
forecast_vs_3m_pct = percentage difference between forecast demand and recent 3-month demand.
Positive values indicate expected growth relative to the recent 3-month baseline.
Negative values indicate expected decline.

## Product Decisions
Products can receive business decision priorities such as:
high_opportunity, opportunity, watch, normal, low_volume_risk, and high_risk.

## Health Status
healthy indicates relatively strong product health.
watch indicates a product that should be monitored.
at_risk indicates elevated business risk.
critical indicates severe risk.

## Opportunity Score
Opportunity score represents the model's estimated business opportunity based on the project's product intelligence signals.

## Risk Score
Risk score represents the estimated business risk based on product intelligence signals.

## Anomaly Detection
Anomalies identify unusual differences between observed and expected product demand.
Anomaly severity indicates the strength of the detected deviation.

## Limitations
Forecasts are model estimates, not guaranteed future sales.
Very low-volume products can produce large percentage changes from small baselines.
The Olist dataset does not provide an authoritative company return policy, shipping policy, or payment policy.
